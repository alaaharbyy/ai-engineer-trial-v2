IT ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY (All staff)

Company laptops and accounts are for business use. Limited personal use is fine if it does not affect work or security.

Never share your password or multi-factor codes with anyone, including IT staff.

Company documents must not be uploaded to personal cloud storage or to public AI tools. Use the approved internal AI assistant for work documents.

Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within one hour so it can be locked remotely.
