LEASE RENEWAL POLICY (Leasing Team)
Effective 1 March 2026

1. Renewal notices. Leasing managers must send a written renewal offer to each tenant no later than 180 days before lease expiry. For anchor tenants occupying more than 2,000 square metres, the offer must go out 270 days before expiry.

2. Rent increases on renewal. The standard renewal increase is capped at 4% over the expiring base rent. Any increase above 4% requires sign-off from the Head of Leasing.

3. Early renewals. A tenant renewing more than 12 months early may be offered one month of free rent, provided the new term is at least five years.

4. Records. Every renewal offer and tenant response is logged in the leasing system within two business days.
