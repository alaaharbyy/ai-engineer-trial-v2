CAPITAL EXPENDITURE APPROVAL MATRIX (Finance)

Capital projects are approved according to their total budgeted cost:

- Up to USD 25,000: Property Manager
- USD 25,001 to 150,000: Regional Director
- USD 150,001 to 750,000: Chief Financial Officer
- Above USD 750,000: Investment Committee

Emergency repairs required for safety may start before approval, but must be submitted for approval within five business days.
Splitting one project into smaller requests to avoid a higher approval level is not allowed.
