TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY (All staff)

Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours; business class is allowed for longer flights.

Hotel nightly limits: USD 220 in major cities, USD 160 elsewhere.

Daily meal allowance: USD 65 per travel day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

Submit expense claims with receipts within 30 days of returning from the trip. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.
