Q3 2026 LENDER REVIEW - CONFIDENTIAL (Finance only)

Summary: all properties met loan covenants in Q3 except Harbor Point (P-104).

Harbor Point fell below its minimum debt service coverage covenant during the quarter because two anchor tenants vacated. Northgate Bank agreed to a one-time covenant waiver in exchange for a waiver fee of USD 185,000 and a cash reserve top-up of USD 400,000, both due by 15 December 2026.

Finance will re-test the covenant at Q4 close. If the coverage remains below the covenant, the bank may require a partial loan paydown.
