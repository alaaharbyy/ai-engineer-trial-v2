ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATE PROCEDURE (Legal, shared with Finance)

An estoppel certificate is a signed statement from a tenant confirming the key terms of its lease. Lenders and buyers request them during financing and sales.

1. Legal prepares the certificate from the lease abstract and sends it to the tenant.
2. Tenants must return the signed certificate within 10 business days. Most leases require this; check the lease clause before chasing.
3. If a tenant has not responded after 7 business days, Legal sends a reminder and copies the property manager.
4. Any tenant comment that disagrees with the lease abstract must be escalated to the General Counsel before the certificate is released to the lender.
5. Certificates are only valid for 30 days after signing.
