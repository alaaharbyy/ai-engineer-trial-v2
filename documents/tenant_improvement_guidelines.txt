TENANT IMPROVEMENT (TI) GUIDELINES (Leasing Team)

Tenant improvement allowances are negotiated per lease and paid only against invoices for completed work.

Standard allowance: up to USD 35 per square metre for new leases of five years or longer. Leases shorter than five years receive no TI allowance unless approved by the Head of Leasing.

Allowances above USD 60 per square metre must also be approved by Finance, because they affect the property's capital budget.

All TI works require a contractor with valid liability insurance and a signed works agreement before any work starts on site.
